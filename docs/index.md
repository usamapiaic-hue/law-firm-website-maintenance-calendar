# Law Firm Website Maintenance Calendar

What to check on a law firm website every week, month, quarter and year, and who should own each task. Published by [Legal Web Agency](https://legalwebagency.com/).

A law firm website rarely breaks loudly. More often a contact form quietly stops sending, a plugin update breaks the mobile menu, or a page keeps describing a law that changed last year. Each of those costs cases without anyone noticing. This calendar is designed so a small firm can catch those problems in about an hour a month. For the background, read our guide to [law firm website maintenance](https://legalwebagency.com/law-firm-website-maintenance/).

## Weekly (10 minutes)

| Check | How | Owner |
|-------|-----|-------|
| Forms deliver | Send a test from a phone; confirm it arrives within a minute and is not in spam | Office manager |
| Phone number works | Tap it on the homepage on a phone | Office manager |
| Site is up | Open the homepage and one practice page | Anyone |
| New reviews answered | Check the Google Business Profile | Attorney or marketing |

The form test matters most. A form that sends from an address your domain is not allowed to send from can be dropped silently by the receiving mail server, so the site looks fine while leads disappear. If your forms send from a free email address, change them to an address on your own domain.

## Monthly (30 minutes)

- **Updates:** apply WordPress core, theme and plugin updates on a staging copy or right after a full backup, then re-test forms and the mobile menu. See [WordPress for law firms](https://legalwebagency.com/wordpress-for-law-firms/).
- **Backups:** confirm a recent backup exists *and* can be restored. Ask your host how. See [law firm website hosting](https://legalwebagency.com/law-firm-website-hosting/).
- **Search Console:** look for new errors, pages dropped from the index, and queries that gained impressions.
- **Leads:** count calls and forms by source. See [conversion tracking for law firms](https://legalwebagency.com/law-firm-website-conversion-tracking/).
- **Reviews:** ask recent clients for reviews with a direct link from the free [Google review link generator](https://legalwebagency.com/google-review-link-generator/).

## Quarterly (1 to 2 hours)

| Area | What to review |
|------|----------------|
| Content accuracy | Laws, fees, deadlines, office hours, staff changes |
| Advertising rules | Results disclaimers, "specialist" claims, testimonials. See [attorney advertising rules by state](https://legalwebagency.com/attorney-advertising-rules-by-state-law-firm-website/) |
| Speed on mobile | Test key pages on a phone; see our [Core Web Vitals study](https://legalwebagency.com/core-web-vitals-law-firm-websites/) |
| Accessibility basics | Alt text, color contrast, form labels, keyboard use. See [is your law firm website ADA compliant?](https://legalwebagency.com/law-firm-website-ada-compliant/) |
| Structured data | Still present after updates. See [schema markup for law firms](https://legalwebagency.com/schema-markup-for-law-firms/) |
| AI visibility | Ask ChatGPT and Google for a lawyer in your practice and city; note whether the firm appears |

## Yearly (half a day)

- Renew the domain and SSL certificate well before expiry, and confirm the domain is registered to the firm, not to a former vendor.
- Review every practice and attorney page; retire or merge pages that no longer reflect the practice.
- Run a full content audit. See the [law firm website content audit](https://legalwebagency.com/law-firm-website-content-audit/).
- Decide whether the site needs a refresh or a full redesign. See the [law firm website redesign guide](https://legalwebagency.com/law-firm-website-redesign-guide/).

## Who should own maintenance

| Firm size | Practical setup |
|-----------|-----------------|
| Solo | Weekly checks yourself; hire help for updates and backups |
| Small firm | Office manager runs weekly and monthly checks; developer on a small retainer for updates |
| Mid-size | Marketing lead owns the calendar; agency or in-house developer handles technical work |
